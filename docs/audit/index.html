How the week runs

No discovery workshops. You send a file, you get a document.

  1. 01

    Send the invoice export

    A CSV or PDF of software charges for the last twelve months. Redact card numbers. We sign an NDA if you need one.

  2. 02

    We price both paths

    Every line gets a replacement shortlist and a real annual cost: licence, hosting, the hours to migrate, and who maintains it after.

  3. 03

    You get the order of operations

    Which two to move first, which to leave alone, and the ones that will cost more to run than they save.
