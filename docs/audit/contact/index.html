Open a request

This button opens a structured request on the public repository, which is an odd place for a commercial conversation but works without a mail server. Do not post financial details there. Set CONTACT_EMAIL in scripts/build_audit.py to switch every button on this site to a private inbox.

What to include

  1. Headcount and roughly how many people have a paid seat per tool.
  2. Approximate annual software spend, a rounded number is fine.
  3. What you suspect is wasteful — the tool everyone complains about, the one nobody remembers buying.
  4. Anything contractual: annual prepay, renewal dates, procurement rules.

Four lines is enough. If it looks like a fit we reply with one scope question and an invoice. If it does not, we say so and tell you why.

What not to send

  • Card numbers, bank details, or anything that would make a breach your problem.
  • System access of any kind. We never need it, and a supplier who asks is worth declining.
  • Your full invoice export before an agreement. Three numbers is enough to start.

Response times

One business day for a first reply. Seven days from a usable export to a delivered document. If the queue is longer than that we say so before you pay, not after.
