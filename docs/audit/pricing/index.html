What the fee covers

Included
The full cost inventory, replacement shortlists, real post-migration pricing, migration sequence, the document, and the call.
Not included
Doing the migration, negotiating with vendors, buying licences on your behalf, or managing anything after delivery.
Turnaround
Seven days from receiving a usable export. If your data arrives messy, we tell you before starting the clock.
Larger companies
Above roughly 100 seats or 40 subscriptions, the work is materially bigger and we quote separately. Usually $1,800–2,400.

Why not a percentage of savings

Because it pays us to recommend bad migrations. A consultant on 20% of savings has a reason to push the riskiest move that shows the biggest number, and no reason to tell you that a replacement will cost more to run than it saves.

A flat fee makes "keep this one" a neutral sentence. It also means our incentive is to finish quickly rather than to keep the engagement open.

The guarantee, honestly

If the document does not identify at least $2,700 of annual savings — three times the fee — we refund half. You keep the document either way, including the analysis of why there was nothing to find.

It is not a full refund because the work was done. Reading twelve months of invoices and pricing replacements takes days whether or not the answer is flattering. A full refund would mean we only get paid when the answer is convenient, which is the incentive we just argued against.

If your stack is under about $15,000 a year, this is probably not worth buying. The maths does not support it and we would rather say so now than take the fee and produce a thin document.

Payment and paperwork

Invoice on acceptance, payable before delivery. Bank transfer or card. We can sign an NDA and your procurement paperwork, but we are a small operation and cannot complete enterprise vendor-security questionnaires that assume a compliance department.
