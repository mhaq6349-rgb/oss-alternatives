Open source alternatives to Retool

Build internal tools by dragging components onto a canvas. If the subscription is the problem, these do the same job and you keep the data.

ToolJet

Clean alternative with a strong component library.

41.1k stars · HTML · AGPL-3.0

Open-source foundation of ToolJet AI - the enterprise app generation platform for internal tools, dashboards, business applications, workflows and AI agents. Build visually, from a prompt, or from Claude Code, Codex and Cursor over MCP 🚀

appsmith

The most direct Retool competitor: drag-and-drop UI over your data.

41.1k stars · TypeScript · Apache-2.0

Platform to build admin panels, internal tools, and dashboards. Integrates with 25+ databases and any API.

budibase

Faster for simple CRUD apps; good for non-developers.

28.4k stars · TypeScript · custom licence

AI agents, automations and apps that run your operations. Model agnostic.
